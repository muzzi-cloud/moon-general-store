MOON GENERAL STORE — WEBSITE DEMO

Included pages:
- index.html       Home page
- shop.html        Product catalogue, search, category filter, sorting and demo bag
- about.html       Brand story
- contact.html     Contact details placeholders and demo contact form
- policies.html    Shipping, returns, privacy, terms and FAQ starter text
- style.css        Shared responsive styling
- script.js        Shared interactive demo cart and product filtering

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Keep the other files in the same folder; the pages share style.css and script.js.

HOW TO PUBLISH
Upload all files together to a static web host. Do not upload just one HTML page, because the other pages and shared files are needed.

IMAGES
Image URLs use Unsplash public image endpoints and require an internet connection. These are illustrative stock photos, not verified images of the listed products. Confirm each image's licence and replace with your own or authorised product photos before a commercial launch.

IMPORTANT
This is a frontend demo. Sample prices and product descriptions are placeholders. The demo cart does not process orders or payments. The contact/newsletter forms do not send data. A real store needs a backend, secure checkout, order/inventory handling, verified business contact details, and complete policies.
